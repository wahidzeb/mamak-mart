# Mamak Mart by Zaib

Mamak Mart is a shop game set in your own little Malaysian mamak. Walk around with one thumb, make teh tarik, roti canai and nasi lemak, carry piles of food to hungry customers, collect coins and grow your shop with new tables, new food and helpers. Survive the breakfast rush and the after-Maghrib rush!

Free to play, no ads during play and nothing to buy. Progress is saved on your device.
